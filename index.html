How To Play

BASICS

Tap a card to flip it. Find two cards with the same symbol to make a match. Match all pairs to win the level.

SCORING

Earn up to 3 stars. Use fewer moves and finish faster for a perfect score.

TIPS

Try to remember card positions. Focus first on finding pairs near each other.
